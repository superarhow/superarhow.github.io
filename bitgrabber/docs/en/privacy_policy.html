Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BitGrabber application (“the App”), a BitTorrent/DHT search and download client. It describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Search and Download Activity

The App lets you search for and download torrents by keyword, info hash, or magnet link. Search queries and info hashes you enter are sent to public BitTorrent trackers and the BitTorrent DHT (Distributed Hash Table) network, and to third-party torrent-indexing services the App queries on your behalf, in order to find peers and results. We do not operate our own tracker or indexing service, and we do not log or store your search history or downloaded content on any server we control.

1.2 Peer-to-Peer Network Data

BitTorrent is a peer-to-peer protocol. When you download or seed a torrent, your device's IP address is necessarily visible to the DHT network, trackers, and other peers in the same swarm — this is how BitTorrent works and is not something the App can hide. The App does not use a VPN or proxy on your behalf. If you want to keep your IP address private from other peers, use a VPN of your own choosing.

1.3 Files and Storage

Downloaded files are saved to a folder on your device that you choose. The App does not upload your downloaded files anywhere; they stay on your device under your control.

1.4 Advertising Data

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded video advertisements. Google may collect and use data from your device to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (country/region)
  • App usage information
  • Non-personal device information (OS version, screen size, etc.)

See Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data practices.

2. How We Use Information

  • To find peers and torrent results for the searches you run.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties beyond the advertising and search/DHT/tracker services described above, which are inherent to how the App and the BitTorrent protocol function.

3. Data Storage and Security

The App does not require an account and does not maintain any cloud storage, user database, or analytics backend of its own. Your settings (download folder, notification preference, etc.) are stored only locally on your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will address it promptly.

5. Your Rights

  • Request information about data practices described here.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Legal Use

The App is a general-purpose BitTorrent client. It does not host, index, or endorse any specific content. You are solely responsible for ensuring that anything you search for, download, or share complies with the copyright and other laws that apply to you.
